A Minecraft mod made in Fabric 1.21 that contains three of the most unique features ever! <br>

1. A TNT Launcher - It launches TNT if it sees TNT in your inventory when you right-click with it in the primary hand or the secondary hand slot!<br>
2. A Bleed effect that pops up on mobs whenever they are hit by the explosion of the TNT Launcher, and when the effect applies to the player, it drains their health immediately, and when <br>
   The player is below 4 hearts, which causes the player to lose consciousness and increases their heartbeat.<br>
3. A Special Food item that may prove to be useful according to different situations - a guacamole!<br>
<br>
Players can use guacamole for escaping mobs in 1x1 tunnels or even from other players in PvP, but it lasts for 30 seconds only, so they will have to make it quick before they<br>
go back to their original size.
